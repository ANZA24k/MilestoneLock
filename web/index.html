FROM AGREEMENT TO APPROVAL

Less ambiguity.
More accountability.

The same standard, for everyone.
No moving the goalposts after delivery.

01

Agree & lock

The sponsor commits the deliverable, criteria, evidence rules and contractor. The contractor accepts that exact commitment.

02

Commit the work

The contractor submits an immutable document URL, exact byte length and SHA-256 hash. Prior evidence stays on record.

03

Verify independently

GenLayer validators retrieve the documents independently, assess every criterion and verify the proposed excerpts.
